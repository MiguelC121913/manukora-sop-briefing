# Manukora S&OP Executive Briefing — March 2026

---

## 1. The Decision

Seven SKUs face stockouts within the next four months, putting **$181,224 in monthly revenue at risk** across the portfolio. Three of those — the MGO 514+ 500g, MGO 850+ 500g, and Bioactive Blend Energy and Recovery — are projected to stock out within two months, meaning they will breach cover before a reorder can arrive if purchase orders are not placed this week. At the same time, four SKUs are overstocked, carrying **$722,788 in excess retail value** that is tying up capital without turning over. The right moves are to place seven reorders immediately and to defer or reduce the inbound purchase order currently heading in for the MGO 100+ 250g.

---

## 2. What Moved This Month

**Accelerating SKUs.** The 500g formats across MGO grades are all gaining momentum. The MGO 263+ 500g is up 8.9% month-over-month since December 2025, with Shopify and Amazon moving in the same direction (+8.3% and +9.8% respectively) — a clean, broad-based signal. The MGO 514+ 500g grew 11.4% month-over-month, and the MGO 850+ 500g grew 8.9%, both with channel-consistent readings. MGO 1700+ 100g is the sharpest mover in the core honey line, up 13.6% month-over-month since December 2025, with Amazon (+14.8%) slightly outpacing Shopify (+12.8%).

**Bioactive Blends launching strongly.** Measured from January 2026 onward (see Assumptions), all three Bioactive Blends are accelerating. Immunity is up 11.9% month-over-month; Energy is up 11.5% with growth essentially matched across Shopify (+11.5%) and Amazon (+11.4%); Recovery is up 13.7%, led by Amazon at +15.6% versus Shopify at +12.5%. The Amazon lead on Recovery is worth watching — if that channel continues to outpace, fulfillment prioritization may matter.

**Channel divergence: MGO 100+ 250g.** This is the one SKU flashing a split signal. Shopify is growing (+3.9% month-over-month) but Amazon is contracting (–4.0%), producing a near-flat blended result. The 250g format is drifting while the 500g counterpart accelerates — a format-mix shift that reinforces the case for deferring further inbound stock.

**Steady compounder: MGO 263+ 250g.** Demand is growing consistently at 5.0% month-over-month since December 2025, with Shopify (+6.7%) ahead of Amazon (+2.5%), but both positive. Well-stocked; no action needed this cycle.

## 5. Judgment Calls

**Propolis Tincture 30ml — controlled exit, not a reorder.** This SKU is suppressed under the Q2 2026 phase-out plan because current cover is still above the 30-day threshold that would trigger a clearance decision. The system correctly set reorder quantity to zero. However, a stockout is still projected within 2 months — this is intentional but it does not manage itself. Channel partners and any retail accounts carrying this SKU should be notified now so they are not caught by an unexpected out-of-stock. Treat this as a comms and sell-through task, not a supply task.

**MGO 100+ 250g — high cover, weakening demand.** This SKU is overstocked and showing a deteriorating demand signal: Amazon is contracting month-over-month while Shopify grows, for a near-flat blended result since December 2025. With 6.2 months of cover on hand and a further 2,000 units inbound, the portfolio is already long on this SKU. The recommendation is to defer or reduce that inbound purchase order rather than let it land and deepen the overstock position. This is a capital-reallocation call — the dollars tied up here are better directed to the 500g accelerators above.

**Pack-size imbalance: MGO 263+ and MGO 514+ grades.** In both cases, the 250g format is comfortably overstocked while the 500g counterpart is critically short:

- MGO 263+: the 250g has over 4.5 months of cover (and 3,200 units inbound, extending that to 6.5 months) while the 500g has 2.49 months of cover with nothing on order.
- MGO 514+: the 250g is overstocked with 3.62 months on hand (5.32 with inbound), while the 500g has just 1.99 months of cover and a 2-month stockout projection.

This is not a per-SKU anomaly — it is a sourcing and allocation pattern. If production runs for these grades are sized or split between formats at origin, the 500g share needs to increase at the next planning conversation with the supplier.

## 6. Assumptions and Caveats

**Lead times.** The default supplier lead time is 2 months. Manuka Honey MGO 1700+ 100g is the only SKU with a non-default lead time: 3 months. All cover and stockout projections for that SKU reflect the longer window; its reorder urgency is higher than its cover-days figure might suggest at first glance.

**Growth cap.** All demand projections are capped at 12% compound monthly growth to prevent unrealistic 12-month extrapolations. Bioactive Blend Energy 250g and Bioactive Blend Recovery 250g both hit this cap — their raw growth rates were 12.2% and 13.2% respectively, and both have been modelled at 12.0%. Actual demand could outpace projections for these SKUs; treat their reorder quantities as a floor, not a ceiling.

**Revenue figures.** All revenue opportunity and revenue-at-risk figures use full retail list price. They do not net out trade discounts, wholesale margins, or promotional pricing. Actual cash impact will be lower, but list price is the correct basis for ranking relative urgency.

**Data quality — Bioactive Blend launch dates.** December 2025 sales were recorded for all three Bioactive Blends despite the business rules specifying January 2026 as the correct trend baseline (these SKUs launched mid-window). December 2025 figures have been excluded from trend and growth calculations for these three SKUs. All trend descriptions in this briefing for the Bioactive Blends reflect January 2026 onward only.
