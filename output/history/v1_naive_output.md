## Strategic Takeaways for Leadership

**1. We have a systemic reorder gap on our fastest-growing SKUs.**
Six SKUs have zero units on order despite demand trends ranging from +26% to +42%. This is not a coincidence — it suggests our reorder triggers are not calibrated for growth. We recommend reviewing and resetting reorder points across the portfolio this quarter.

**2. The Bioactive Blend line is our highest-growth category and our most exposed.**
All three Bioactive SKUs are growing 36–42% per quarter. Two of three have no replenishment plan. If this category is a strategic priority (and the growth suggests it should be), it needs inventory investment to match.

**3. The 500g format is systematically underinvested.**
MGO 263+ 500g, 514+ 500g, and 850+ 500g are all critical, all have zero units on order, and all are growing faster than their 250g counterparts. Customers are trading up in pack size — we should be actively supporting (not accidentally starving) that behavior.

**4. Total at-risk revenue in the next 60 days.**
Across the six critical SKUs, projected 2-month revenue at risk is approximately **$263,532** if stockouts occur. Reorder investment to cover these SKUs is a straightforward ROI decision.

- [ ] Review and reset reorder point triggers across all SKUs based on current growth rates, not historical averages
