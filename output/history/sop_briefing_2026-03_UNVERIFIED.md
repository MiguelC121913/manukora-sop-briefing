## Data Notes

The engine flagged a data inconsistency for the three Bioactive Blend SKUs: all three show December 2025 sales in the source data despite being designated as mid-window launches with a later trend baseline. Rather than silently include December figures in growth and trend calculations — which would have distorted the demand picture — the system excluded December 2025 from trend analysis for these SKUs. All Bioactive Blend trend and growth figures in this briefing reflect M2 (January 2026) onward only. This was a deliberate, logged exclusion, not a data gap.
