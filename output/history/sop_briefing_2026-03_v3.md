## 1. The Decision

Seven SKUs need reorders this cycle, with **$181,224** in monthly revenue at risk if purchase orders are not placed promptly — several of those SKUs will stock out within two months. Against that urgency, the portfolio is simultaneously carrying **$722,788** in excess retail inventory, concentrated in a handful of overstocked 250g lines that are not the same SKUs facing stockouts. The imbalance is not a contradiction; it is a capital-allocation signal: money is sitting in the wrong pack sizes. Approve the seven reorders, defer or reduce the inbound purchase order on MGO 100+ 250g, and take no action on Propolis Tincture, which is exiting the range on a controlled timeline.

## 2. What Moved This Month

**Accelerating lines.** The 500g format across MGO grades is the clearest momentum story this period. MGO 263+ 500g has grown demand consistently from December 2025 through March 2026, with Shopify (+8.3%) and Amazon (+9.8%) moving in the same direction and at comparable rates — a clean, channel-broad signal. MGO 514+ 500g is accelerating faster still, up 11.4% month-on-month with near-identical Shopify (+11.8%) and Amazon (+10.8%) readings. MGO 850+ 500g and MGO 1700+ 100g are similarly accelerating, with Amazon slightly outpacing Shopify on both — Amazon running at +14.8% for MGO 1700+ 100g against Shopify's +12.8% suggests Amazon search and subscribe-and-save are driving the premium end harder than direct.

The Bioactive Blend range — Immunity, Energy, and Recovery — is accelerating meaningfully from their January 2026 baselines (December 2025 data is excluded from trend calculations for these three SKUs; see Assumptions). All three show Shopify and Amazon moving in the same direction, with Recovery's Amazon channel (+15.6%) running notably ahead of its Shopify channel (+12.5%) — worth watching as a potential channel-mix shift.

**Stalling and diverging lines.** MGO 100+ 250g is the portfolio's clearest warning. Shopify is growing (+3.9%) but Amazon is declining (-4.0%) — a split that produces a near-flat overall trend. This is not a rounding error; the two channels are moving in opposite directions. MGO 263+ 250g and MGO 850+ 250g are steady growers, both channels positive and aligned.

## 5. Judgment Calls

**Controlled exit — Propolis Tincture 30ml.** The reorder that the engine computed for this SKU has been suppressed under the Q2 2026 phase-out plan, because current cover (41 days) is still above the 30-day threshold that triggers exit. This is a deliberate business decision, not a system error. However, a stockout is still projected in month 2 — which means this is not a passive wind-down. Channel communications should be planned now (retailer notifications, Amazon listing management, Shopify delisting timeline) so the stockout lands as a managed exit rather than an unexpected out-of-stock. Do not reorder; do manage the customer-facing transition actively.

**Overstocked with flattening demand — MGO 100+ 250g.** This SKU is flagged for executive review rather than a standard reorder. It is overstocked (6,258 excess units, $156,387 in excess retail value tied up) *and* showing a split demand signal: Shopify growing modestly, Amazon declining. The overall trend is stalling. Separately, 2,000 units are already inbound on a purchase order. The recommendation is to defer or reduce that inbound PO — adding more stock into a channel where demand is contracting on Amazon would deepen the capital exposure without growing into it. This warrants a direct conversation with the buyer or channel team about what is driving the Amazon softness before any further inventory commitment is made.

**Pack-size imbalance within the same MGO grade.** Two grade pairings deserve explicit attention:

- **MGO 263+:** The 250g format has 4.55 months of cover (with an inbound PO, 6.55 months) and carries $247,869 in excess inventory. The 500g format has 2.49 months of cover, no units on order, a month-3 stockout, and needs 1,500 units urgently. The same grade is simultaneously overstocked in one pack size and critically short in the other.
- **MGO 514+:** The 250g format has 3.62 months of cover (5.32 with inbound PO) and $149,070 in excess inventory. The 500g format has 1.99 months of cover, no units on order, and a month-2 stockout requiring 1,200 units.

In both cases, the sourcing and allocation process has been over-weighting 250g at the expense of 500g. This is worth reviewing at the PO-planning level — not just reacting to the individual SKU signals but asking whether the split between pack sizes at the same grade is being set intentionally.

## 6. Assumptions and Caveats

**Lead times.** The default supplier lead time used across most SKUs is 2 months. MGO 1700+ 100g is the exception, carrying a 3-month lead time — which is why its month-4 stockout projection still triggers an immediate reorder recommendation. The default cover target is also 2 months, with MGO 1700+ 100g again the exception at a 3-month target.

**Growth rate cap.** Monthly growth rates are capped at 12% per month for forward projections. Bioactive Blend Energy 250g and Bioactive Blend Recovery 250g both hit this ceiling — their raw compound growth rates were higher. Reorder quantities for these two SKUs are therefore based on a conservative demand projection; actual demand could arrive faster. All other SKUs in this cycle are below the cap and reflect uncapped growth rates.

**Revenue figures.** All revenue figures in this briefing use full list retail prices and are not net of discounts, promotions, or wholesale margins. Actual realised revenue will be lower.

**Bioactive Blend data quality.** December 2025 sales data exists for all three Bioactive Blend SKUs (Immunity, Energy, Recovery) but has been excluded from their trend and growth calculations. These products launched mid-window, and their December 2025 figures are inconsistent with the later-month baselines set in business rules. Trend and growth for these SKUs are computed from January 2026 onward only. The December 2025 figures are not missing — they are present in the source data but deliberately excluded to avoid distorting the growth trajectory.
